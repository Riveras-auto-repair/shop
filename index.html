Where to find your tire size

Look at the side of any tire on your car. You'll see a code like 205/55R16. Type it into the quote form and we can price your tires faster.

205
Width of the tire in millimeters
/55
Height of the sidewall, as a percent of the width
R16
Fits a 16 inch wheel
